- The Attempt Log heading shows total recorded playtime across all categories. Each boss card shows its own playtime in the selected category.
- Open **Overall Progress** with the **+** beside the Attempt Log tabs. The graph overlays color-coded boss trends for the selected category, with a boss icon legend, animated zoom and panning, and percentage-based smoothing. Each trend spans its full clear history, and the 0% reference represents that boss’s average clear time.
- Turn on **Show boss average** to overlay a combined trend and dim individual boss lines. Hover over the graph to see overall improvement and the death percentage at that point.
- Deaths now appear alongside clears and failures in each boss’s **Attempts** tab and can be deleted individually.
