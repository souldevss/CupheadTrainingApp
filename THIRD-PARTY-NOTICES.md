# Credits and third-party components

- The web interface is based on [souldevss/CupheadTrainingTable](https://github.com/souldevss/CupheadTrainingTable).
- The memory-reading engine was adapted from the accompanying CupheadMusicPlayer project. Its existing namespaces and source comments are retained in `Engine/`.
- Reference times, boss icons, and some sounds link to [myekul.com](https://myekul.com/).
- The card frame and death-screen portraits are Cuphead artwork by Studio MDHR. The frame was supplied by the user; portraits came from the user’s downloaded [Death Cards archive](https://www.spriters-resource.com/pc_computer/cupheaddontdealwiththedevil/asset/97983/).
- The title and boss-name fallback font is Alegreya Sans SC. Its SIL Open Font License is included in `Web/assets/fonts/alegreyasanssc-OFL.txt`.
- [Microsoft.Web.WebView2](https://www.nuget.org/packages/Microsoft.Web.WebView2/) and [Microsoft.Data.Sqlite](https://www.nuget.org/packages/Microsoft.Data.Sqlite/) are restored through NuGet. Their dependencies retain their respective licenses.
